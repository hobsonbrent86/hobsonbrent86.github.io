    Greater Tampa Bay Area

    Wytebox

    Chief Executive Officer & Founder

    Founded the company and run it. Positioning, roadmap, and P&L sit in one place.

    Wytebox is an enterprise context layer for AI agents. It captures how work happens without connectors, maps that work to projects and goals, and exposes the context through MCP. The product is usable from a command line and in plain language.

    Architected it from scratch, including retrieval, persistent data, and a fleet of agents for revenue and optimization. Shipped the production system in Python. Distributed it through MDM, hands-on with Jamf, Intune, Automox, and NinjaOne. The data layer runs on Supabase.
